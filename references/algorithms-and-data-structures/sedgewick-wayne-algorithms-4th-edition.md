# Algorithms, 4th Edition - Graphs

Sedgewick, R., & Wayne, K. (2011).

*Algorithms* (4th ed.).

Addison-Wesley Professional.

[Graphs - Algorithms, 4th Edition](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/40graphs/)

**Topics:** Graph representations, undirected graphs, directed graphs, depth-first search, breadth-first search, connected components, minimum spanning trees, shortest paths

**Used in:** Graph theory foundations, BFS, DFS, connected graphs and disconnected components, minimum spanning trees, and shortest-path algorithm study

**Notes:** Used as a supplementary textbook and online reference while studying graph structures and graph algorithms. The visual examples and implementations are particularly useful for comparing theoretical graph concepts with their practical algorithmic behavior.
